# HomeFit Plan — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: October 30, 2026*

**The short version: HomeFit Plan collects no data. None.** The app has no account, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and, if you choose, in your own iCloud.

## What the app stores

- **Your profile:** birth year, sex, height, goal, experience level, equipment, training schedule and any body areas you asked the app to avoid.
- **Your plans and workouts:** the exercises in each plan, the sets, reps and weights you log, how each workout felt, and the dates.
- **Body entries:** the weight and tape measurements you choose to record.
- **Settings:** units, reminder times and whether the plan is paused.

This is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled, it is also stored in the private part of *your* iCloud account, so a new iPhone picks up where the old one left off. Apple encrypts that data in transit and at rest; it is never visible to us and is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Turning off iCloud for HomeFit Plan in iOS Settings keeps everything on the device only.

## What the app does not do

- No data collection of any kind, personal or otherwise
- No network requests to us, no telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting
- No third-party SDKs, trackers or advertising
- No account or sign-in
- No access to Apple Health, contacts, photos or location

## Purchases

HomeFit Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app never sees your payment details. The only thing it keeps about a purchase is whether Pro is unlocked on your device, which it checks with Apple's StoreKit. Refunds and purchase history are managed by Apple.

## Notifications

Workout reminders and the weekly weigh-in nudge are local notifications scheduled on your device. They exist only if you turn them on, and nothing about them leaves the phone.

## Backups you export

*Export data* writes a JSON file with your profile, plans, workouts and body entries to a location you choose. That file is yours; where you keep it is up to you. *Import data* reads such a file back and replaces the current data.

## Deleting your data

Settings → *Reset everything* removes all of your data from the device and, if iCloud is on, from your iCloud account. Deleting the app removes its data from the device; data in iCloud can also be removed from iOS Settings → your Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

## Children

HomeFit Plan is not directed at children under 13 and, because it collects no data, holds no personal information about anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date, and the App Store privacy label will reflect it.
